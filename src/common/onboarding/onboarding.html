One-minute setup

Your protection is ready

Allow the supported sites once, then AwayDoomscrollin' can interrupt distracting short-form feeds automatically.

01

Allow supported sites

Access is limited to Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube so the extension can recognize protected routes and display its local intervention.

No access to other websites is requested.

02

Your impact statistics stay local

The extension counts interventions separately for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. This version stores those counters only in this browser and sends no telemetry.

Read the complete privacy disclosure ↗
03

Choose the platforms to protect

Protection is targeted: only the distracting routes described below are interrupted, while intentional areas remain available.

You can change each platform setting later from the extension menu.
